TECHHUB LTD OPERATION AGREEMENT
Between
THE FACULTY OF LAW, SAMPLE UNIVERSITY
and
TECHHUB LTD
Commencement Date: ______________________

PREAMBLE
This Hub Operation Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Commencement Date set out
above by and between:
• The Faculty of Law of Sample University being a partner ("the Faculty"); and
• TechHub LTD, a technology enterprise duly registered in Nigeria ("the Operator" or
"TechHub").
The Faculty and TechHub are hereinafter collectively referred to as "the Parties" and individually as
a "Party."

1. PURPOSE
This Agreement defines the terms under which the Faculty grant TechHub the exclusive right to
operate a technology hub, co-working space, and digital training center within the Faculty of Law
premises. The parties intend this arrangement to benefit students and the wider university community
while enabling TechHub to operate a sustainable commercial enterprise.

2. DEFINITIONS
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:
• "Commencement Date" means the date first written above on the cover page of this Agreement;
• "Premises" means the physical space within the Faculty of Law described in Schedule A attached hereto;
• "TechHub Infrastructure" means all solar panels, batteries, inverters, internet equipment, furniture, and other assets installed by TechHub;
• "Revenue" means workspace fees received by TechHub from users of the Premises, excluding VAT and other applicable taxes;
• "University Community" means currently enrolled students, academic staff, and administrative staff of the University;
• "NDPA" means the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and any successor legislation;
• "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond a party's reasonable control, including but not limited to industrial strikes, campus closure, fire, flood, or acts of government, Civil & Political Unrest, acts of God.

3. LEASED PREMISES
The faculty grants the operator exclusive use of the designated space within the Faculty of Law as
described and delineated in Schedule A (the "Premises"). Schedule A, which forms an integral part of
this Agreement, shall specify:
• The exact floor plan and dimensions of the Premises;
• The location within the Faculty of Law building;
• Any shared or common areas to which the operator shall have access; and
• Any areas expressly excluded from the grant of use.

4. TERM
This Agreement shall run for three (3) years commencing on the Commencement Date and expiring on
the third anniversary thereof, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of this
Agreement.

4.1 Renewal Priority
The operator shall have the first right of refusal to renew this Agreement for a further term of three (3)
years on terms to be negotiated in good faith, subject to:
• Written notice of intention to renew given not less than six (6) months before expiry;
• Satisfactory performance assessed against the criteria set out in Schedule B; and
• Mutual agreement on any revised financial terms.

5. EXCLUSIVITY
During the term of this Agreement, the Faculty shall not:
• Operate or permit any third party to operate a competing technology hub, co-working space,
or digital training centre within the Faculty of Law building or within [50] metres of the Premises; or
• Grant any rights to any third party that would materially interfere with TechHub's operations
under this Agreement.

6. PERMITTED USE
The Premises shall be used for:
• Co-working and productivity services;
• Electronic library (E-Library) for law students;
• Internet and electricity services;
• Tech training and academy programs;
• Workshops, events, and innovation activities

7. FINANCIAL TERMS
7.1 Revenue Share
In consideration of the grant of exclusive use of the Premises, TechHub shall remit to the
university/faculty twenty-five percent (25%) of workspace revenue earned in each calendar month within
fifteen (15) business days of the end of that month.
For the avoidance of doubt, Revenue share shall apply to co-working and workspace fees.

7.2 Reporting
TechHub shall provide the Faculty with monthly operational and revenue reports within ten (15)
business days after the end of each month through its dedicated digital management platform and
supporting written documentation, where necessary.

8. INSTALLATION & OWNERSHIP OF EQUIPMENT
The operator shall have the right to install, at its own cost and risk, solar panels, batteries, internet
equipment, furniture, and any other equipment reasonably necessary for the permitted use.

8.1 Ownership Protection
All equipment installed by the operator shall remain the sole and exclusive property of TechHub at
all times.

8.2 Removal Rights
Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement:
• The operator shall have sixty (60) days to remove all installed equipment;
• Removal shall be carried out so as to cause no permanent structural damage to the Premises.

9. ACCESS & OPERATIONAL RIGHTS
The operator shall have reasonable daily access to the Premises, including weekends and public
holidays, subject only to bona fide security or emergency closures of the university with reasonable
prior notice where practicable. Any planned maintenance or closure affecting the premises shall be
communicated in writing to TechHub at least fourteen (15) days in advance.

15. DATA OWNERSHIP & PROTECTION
All user and customer data generated through TechHub services on the premises shall remain the
sole property of TechHub, subject to the following:
• TechHub shall comply fully with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 (NDPR) and
any successor legislation;
• TechHub shall maintain a lawful basis for all data processing activities;
• TechHub shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to
protect personal data;
• TechHub shall not sell or transfer personal data of University Community members to third
parties without their informed consent.

--- Page 7 ---
16.3 Termination Without Cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days'
written notice to the other Parties.

18. EXIT / BUYOUT
If, during the term of this Agreement, the University or Faculty wishes to take over the operation of
the hub, it shall give TechHub not less than six (6) months' prior written notice of its intention.

19. GOVERNANCE
The Parties shall meet not less than once every six (6) months to review operations, address any
concerns, and discuss the development of the relationship.

22. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal
Republic of Nigeria. The Parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Nigeria for
any matter not referred to arbitration.
